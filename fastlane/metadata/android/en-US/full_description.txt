Dicom Viewer is a free, native DICOM viewer for medical image files — exactly what you need to open the scan CD or USB stick you were handed after a hospital visit.

🩻 REAL DICOM SUPPORT
Direct support for the DICOM format, including compressed (JPEG, JPEG Lossless) and uncompressed images, and multi-frame cine loops such as cardiac catheterizations.

📽️ CINE PLAYBACK
Play multi-frame series like a video, with adjustable speed (5–30 fps), a frame slider, and step-by-step navigation.

🎚️ WINDOW/LEVEL PRESETS
For CT images: quickly switch between Abdomen, Lung, Bone, and Brain windowing, recomputed from the original raw pixel values via Rescale Slope/Intercept.

📚 SERIES NAVIGATOR
Files from the same series (Series Instance UID) are grouped automatically, so a whole folder of loose slices shows up as one tidy series.

📤 EXPORT FRAMES
Export any single frame as PNG or JPEG and share it directly.

💶 FREE
No subscription, no paywall. A voluntary tip jar is available for anyone who wants to support the project.

Dicom Viewer sends nothing to a server — everything stays on your own device.
